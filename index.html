OUTCRY ...back from the dead...

Formed in 1997 in Germany by Dave G. & ACiDicy, Outcry is first and foremost a dynamic electronic live act and DJ duo. Rooted in the raw energy of early 90s Hip-Hop and UK Hardcore, their true home has always been the stage and the club. While they released several breakbeat singles over the years, their 2009 concept album "Hot Gush" was a deliberate detour—an atmospheric, soundtrack-influenced project that temporarily stepped away from their explosive live sound.

​

Now, in 2026, Outcry is realigning those tracks with their true DNA. The upcoming album "Eruption" completely reimagines the cinematic material of their past, transforming it into the high-octane, unyielding breakbeat energy that defines their live performances. Outcry is taking the music out of the concept and bringing it back to the dancefloor.
